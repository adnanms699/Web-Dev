My take on GPA pressure:

Everyone tells you "Have a good GPA"
"Get a 4.0" But what if I tell you GPA isn't the whole thing?
GPA isn't the only thing that matters to get a job and the situation of the job market now,
"It's beyond cooked"

In today's job market it's rarely just about the degree or the GPA.

Here's what Companies are looking for:
✅ Great communication skills
✅ Storytelling
✅ Great Attitude
✅ Your Uniqueness
And with AI storming the job market, you can't just sit back and say, "I have a good GPA."

Most people with a 4.0 lack communication skill.
